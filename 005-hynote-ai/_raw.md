HyNote AI feels like a product built for someone who actually works in knowledge-intensive roles. It's a full stack solution, meaning it doesn't just record meetings—it pulls from PDFs, screenshots, voice notes, video snippets, and weaves them all together into a unified note system. I tested it with a research workflow where I had meeting recordings, PDF whitepapers, and phone call audio all happening in a single project. What struck me was the cross-device sync.

I took a note on my iPhone using voice during a commute, and by the time I opened my Mac, the note was there with a cleaned-up transcript and preliminary summary waiting. The clean, Apple Notes-like design is deceptively powerful—it doesn't show off, it just works. And the "chat with notes" capability is genuinely useful when you need to dig into research from three weeks ago without rewatching everything.

What needs improvement

Here's where I need to be direct: the payment experience is rough. One reviewer mentioned getting double-charged after accidentally clicking upgrade, and HyNote's refund process apparently pointed them to App Store links when they paid directly on the website. That's a customer service failure that should not happen, especially when dealing with billing. Beyond that, template options for business use are sparse.

If you're managing client deliverables, you want to generate diagrams or structured charts from your notes, and HyNote doesn't quite give you those options yet. The interface is minimal (which I love), but it means fewer customization options. Some professionals would want more visual structure options for their notes.

vs Alternatives

I compared it against HyNote's direct competitors like Voicenotes and Jamie. Jamie seems great if all you care about is meeting summaries—it's hyper-focused and probably does that one thing better. But HyNote's breadth is what appeals to me. You can feed it literally any content source—your Apple Watch recordings, Notion documents, Google Drive files—and it tries to make sense of it all together.

That ecosystem play is powerful. Voicenotes is lighter and faster for quick voice notes, but HyNote handles the complex workflows that come with managing research projects or ongoing client work. The Apple Watch integration especially feels forward-thinking; capturing thoughts on the wrist and having them sync is genuinely convenient.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to help your team unlock HyNote's full potential.

What is HyNote?

HyNote is an AI note-taking app that turns meetings, audio, PDFs, videos, and links into searchable notes. It offers instant summarization, meeting transcription, multimodal capture (8 input types), audio-to-text in 50+ languages, productivity integrations, and encrypted compliance-ready storage. Trusted by 1M+ professionals and students worldwide.

How does HyNote work?

Capture content—record a live meeting, upload audio/video/PDF, or paste a YouTube or web link. HyNote's AI Core Engine transcribes speech (up to 99% accuracy for clear audio), applies instant summarization with key points and action items, and outputs structured notes via 30+ professional templates. Export to Google Docs, Notion, PDF, or TXT—usually in under a minute.

How does HyNote instant summarization work?

HyNote uses abstractive summarization—not just keyword extraction—to read full transcripts or documents and generate concise summaries, key points, action items, and meeting minutes. Output formats include brief summaries, structured meeting notes, study notes, and 30+ professional templates (SWOT, interviews, follow-up emails). Results are ready in seconds and can be exported to PDF, TXT, Google Docs, or Notion.

What types of content can I use with HyNote?

HyNote supports eight multimodal inputs: (1) live audio recording for meetings and lectures; (2) uploaded audio/video in MP3, WAV, M4A, and AAC; (3) PDF documents for summarization and chat; (4) images with OCR for whiteboards and slides; (5) YouTube links for video transcripts and summaries; (6) web page capture; (7) phone call recordings; and (8) Apple Watch recordings. Each input is processed into a transcript, summary, or both.

Frequently Asked Questions
What plans does HyNote offer?

HyNote offers five plans: Free, Pro, Plus, Unlimited, and Teams. Free costs $0 with no credit card required. Paid plans can be billed monthly or annually, and you can cancel at any time. Monthly billing: Pro $11.99/month, Plus $18.99/month, Unlimited $24.99/month, Teams $20 per seat/month. Annual billing: Pro $79.99/year ($6.66/month), Plus $129.99/year ($10.83/month), Unlimited $189.99/year ($15.83/month), Teams $180 per seat/year ($15 per seat/month).

What is included in the HyNote Free plan?

The Free plan includes audio recording and transcription, professional templates, file uploads, note sync across web, mobile, and tablet apps, calendar integration, and private secured storage. No credit card is required to get started.

Do paid plans come with a free trial?

Yes, the Pro, Plus, and Unlimited plans each include a 7-day free trial before billing begins.

How much can I save with annual billing?

Annual billing saves up to 45% compared to monthly pricing, depending on the plan you choose.

What does the Unlimited plan include that other plans do not?

The Unlimited plan adds unlimited real-time audio transcription, recording sessions up to 3 hours (the same as Plus), unlimited private and secure storage, and CRM integration on top of everything in the Plus plan.

What is the HyNote Teams plan?

The Teams plan starts at 2 seats. It costs $20 per seat per month with monthly billing, or $180 per seat per year ($15 per seat per month) with annual billing. It includes everything in the Unlimited plan plus team templates, admin controls, and priority support.

Can I export my notes or transfer them to other apps?

Pro plan users can export transcripts, while Plus and above also support transferring notes directly to Google Docs, Notion, and other platforms.

Which languages does HyNote's live transcription support?

HyNote's web live transcription supports English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese. The HyNote mobile app supports more languages, so you can transcribe a wider range of languages directly from your phone.
