What's great

Tried ElevenLabs recently for voiceovers and honestly the realism is impressive. The emotional tone and pauses sound much more natural compared to most TTS tools I’ve tested. I especially liked how easy the API integration and multilingual support were for experimenting with AI agent workflows and narration projects.

One thing I noticed is that long-form consistency can still vary slightly, especially with numbers or certain pronunciations, but overall it feels like one of the strongest voice AI products out there right now.

What needs improvement

The biggest improvement area is consistency in longer generations. Sometimes the tone, pronunciation, or pacing shifts slightly between outputs, especially with numbers, abbreviations, or non-English words. More fine-grained control over accents and pronunciation handling would make the platform even stronger for multilingual and professional production workflows.

vs Alternatives

I also explored alternatives like Amazon Polly, Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, and PlayHT. While those platforms are solid for basic TTS use cases, ElevenLabs stood out because the voices felt significantly more natural and expressive, especially for storytelling, narration, and AI agent interactions. The balance between voice quality, ease of use, and API flexibility made it the better choice for my experiments.

Best text-to-speech for production use.

The voice quality is unmatched—natural, expressive, and convincing. That said, it's not perfect yet: voice tone consistency across multiple calls/sessions can vary.
